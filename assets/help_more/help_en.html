Q: How Productive works? more... less...
A: Productive helps you build habits. Plan your days, track what you have and haven't done each day, and celebrate great results.
On the "Today" screen, you can see the habits planned for today and the current part of the day.
Swipe right to mark your habits as "done". Mark all the day's habits that are due as "done" to get a "perfect" day. Building up streaks of "perfect" days will keep you motivated and one step closer to a better life.

Q: How streaks work? more... less...
A: Each time you mark a habit as "done" without missing it, your streak for that habit goes up. Skipping a habit will not break your streak, though won't increase it either. If you miss a habit (i.e. you don't mark it as "done" or "skipped"), this will break your streak.
If a habit is scheduled for both morning and evening you need to complete it both to get a streak count.
Mark all due habits for the day as "done", get a "perfect" day and watch your streaks grow in Statistics.

Q: When to use skipping? more... less...
A: You should only skip a habit for a good reason, e.g. you can't put the bin out while on vacation. Skipping doesn't break your streak, but it will prevent you from getting a perfect day.
If a habit is due on a particular day and you don't mark it as "done" or "skipped", it is automatically marked as "missed". Missed habits break your streak, and result in an "overdue" message the next time the habit is due.
There is no need to skip weekly or monthly habit on the days that you don't do it.

Q: Tracking bad habits more... less...
A: Bad habits can be tracked just like good habits.
Let's say you wanted to limit junk food to two days a week. Just add a five-times-a-week habit for "No junk food", and then mark it as 'done' on the days when you don't eat junk food.

Q: How do I edit the past days? more... less...
A: You can bring up past days by tapping on the date at the top of "Today" screen, use the calendar in Statistics or habit details (by tapping a habit).
Keep in mind: You can only edit habits from the day they were created.

Q: How reminders work? more... less...
A: You can set general reminders in Menu->Reminders to get a plan for each part of the day and a summary at the end of the day.
You can also add timed reminders for individual habits. These reminders will only be sent on days when the habit appears on your list, and if you haven't already marked it as "done" or "skipped".

Productive Premium is a paid subscription that gives you access to unlimited habits.

End of trial and subscription renewal
When the trial period ends, Google Play automatically initiates billing against the credit card that the user provided during the initial purchase, at the amount set for the full subscription, and continuing at the subscription interval. If necessary, you can cancel the subscription at any time during the trial period. In this case, the subscription remains active until the end of the trial period, but Google Play sets the subscription not to renew automatically; at the end of the trial period the subscription expires, and Google Play does not charge you.

Subscription Cancellation

Subscriptions on Google Play renew automatically unless you unsubscribe. Uninstalling an app will not automatically stop your subscription. You can view the status of all of your subscriptions and cancel them if necessary by going to the Google Play Store and tapping Menu > Subscriptions. You can also access this page directly through the link http://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions or by using a computer to visit play.google.com and clicking My subscriptions in the menu on the left.

When you cancel a subscription, Google Play does not offer a refund for the current billing cycle. Instead, it allows the user to have access to the canceled subscription until the end of the current billing cycle, at which time it terminates the subscription.

Uninstall app

When you decide to uninstall an app that includes purchased subscriptions, the Play Store app will notify you about the active subscriptions. If you choose to continue with the uninstallation, the app will be removed but the subscriptions will remain active and recurring billing will continue. You can cancel the associated subscriptions at any time in My apps & games screen of the Play Store app.

Notifications

You may turn notifications off at any time in Settings > Notification > App Notifications. Tap the app Productive and then tap the toggle to block push-notifications
